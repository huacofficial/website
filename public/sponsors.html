Partner With HUAC

HUAC is a collection of Harvard's most passionate engineers and scientists, dedicated to advancing the aerospace industry. Our four sub-teams — Rocketry, Satellite, UAV, and Operations — each drive innovation and technical excellence.

Recent funding cuts have affected student organizations across campus, including HUAC. As we continue developing cutting-edge aerospace projects, we seek industry partners who share our vision for cultivating the next generation of aerospace leaders.

Monetary Support

Your financial support directly enables hands-on aerospace engineering at Harvard.

Primary Funding Priorities: machining and equipment costs, critical hardware purchases, transportation to rocketry testing facilities, RF components for satellite ground station development.

Additional Support Opportunities: travel and lodging for the Cape Canaveral launch trip, summer project operations, and aerospace conference networking events to connect students with industry professionals.

Non-Monetary Support

We value strategic partnerships beyond funding.

We seek mentorship from industry experts and academic professionals to support our satellite, rocketry, and UAV teams through design reviews, systems guidance, and mission planning.

We welcome partnerships to host technical workshops, networking events, career development sessions, resume reviews, and recruiting opportunities to create meaningful pathways between Harvard students and the aerospace industry.

Corporate Support Tiers

Moon Tier 1 — Crew Sponsor

Entry-level sponsorship. Recognition via Instagram story; opportunity to recruit via email outreach and résumé collection.

Planet Tier 2 — System Sponsor

$1,000+. All Tier 1 benefits, plus a 2"×2" logo on team shirts, LinkedIn & Instagram recognition, virtual recruiting meetings, website mention, small logo on the Mojave Sphinx rocket, and live test invitations for Rocketry & UAVs.

Star Tier 3 — Mission Partner

$5,000+. All Tier 2 benefits, plus a large logo on team shirts, a dedicated social media feature, an open invitation for an in-person visit, prominent website placement, campus-wide event partnership, a 4"×4" logo on the Mojave Sphinx rocket, panel speaking opportunities, and a branded launch video.

Constellation Tier — Premier Partner

$10,000+. All Tier 3 benefits, plus custom project collaborations and branded projects with the team.

Galaxy Tier — Title Partner: $25,000+. Open to discussion.

All sponsorship benefits and equivalent-value estimates are valid for one year from the date sponsorship is received.
