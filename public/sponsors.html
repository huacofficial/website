Why sponsor HUAC?

Your support moves student-built aerospace forward.

HUAC brings together students building rockets, operating a satellite mission, and developing an autonomous UAV. Sponsorship helps turn ambitious engineering into hardware that can be tested, flown, and learned from.

Partners can connect with emerging engineers through recruiting, technical talks, design reviews, and other opportunities shaped around shared goals.

2026-27 priorities

What your partnership makes possible

Rocketry

Design, build, and launch Harvard's first 10,000-foot IREC COTS rocket. Advance liquid-propulsion research through static fires and cold-flow tests, and expand Level 1 and Level 2 certification opportunities.

Satellite

Continue HUCSat mission operations, data analysis, and ground-station activities while developing future CubeSat concepts and payload technologies.

UAV

Build and evaluate the V2 unmanned aerial vehicle for the VFS Design-Build-Vertical-Flight competition, including the hardware, travel, and flight testing needed to compete.

Support also funds: rocket materials, avionics, recovery systems, satellite components, UAV hardware, manufacturing, safety, and competition travel.
